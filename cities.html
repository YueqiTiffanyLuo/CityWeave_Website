311 Live

No Category List. No Form. Just Show It and Talk.

Residents open 311 Live, point their camera at the problem and talk. A live AI agent sees the scene, hears them and knows the location.

It asks only what the photo can't show, writes a complete service request and suggests the department. The resident confirms it, and 311 staff get a ticket a crew can act on the first time.

It sits on top of the 311 system a city already runs.

How It Works

Like Having a Real Conversation

The agent sees through the camera and talks with you out loud.

AgentI see loose trash and bags partly blocking the sidewalk. Is this what you'd like to report?

ResidentYes, I just saw it today.

AgentCan people using wheelchairs still pass, or is it completely blocked?

ResidentA wheelchair user would have trouble getting through.

AgentThanks. I've written up the report. Please check it before I send it.
