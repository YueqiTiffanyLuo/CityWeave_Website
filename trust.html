Transparency

What Is DTPR?

DTPR stands for Digital Trust for Places and Routines. Think of it as a nutrition label for technology.

It is an open-source standard, stewarded by Helpful Places, made of simple icons and plain definitions. They explain what a system collects, why, who is accountable and what rights you have. Boston and Washington, D.C. are among the places that have piloted it.

How It Builds Trust

People Can See the System

Invisible systems become something anyone can follow.

The Language Is Shared

The same icons mean the same thing from place to place.

Questions Get Easier

Residents and staff can talk from the same explanation.
