Fix It on the First Visit

Property

Less Back and Forth. Fewer Hours Lost.

A vague maintenance request often means two trips: a first visit to diagnose the problem, and a second with the right tools and parts. Each one consumes technician hours, travel time and scheduling with the tenant.

With CityWeave Tech, tenants show the problem and describe it aloud. The agent produces a work order in technical language, so your team can prepare before anyone is dispatched and make the first visit the repair.

How It Works

The Agent Asks

Where is the water coming from? Is it still flowing? Is it near anything electrical?

It Creates

A technical work order with photos, unit and entry notes.

It Saves

Diagnostic trips, technician hours and repeated coordination with tenants.
