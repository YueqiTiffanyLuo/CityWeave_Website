Every city problem starts as a story.

We're building a shorter path from what someone experiences to what a team receives.

It started on a sidewalk.

One morning in the East Village, a big pile of trash blocked the sidewalk. There was a school right ahead, and an older neighbor in a wheelchair couldn't get past.

So we did what you're supposed to do and opened the 311 app. It guessed a category, got it wrong, and offered a form to fill out by hand on the way to work.

The form never asked whether a wheelchair could get through. That question became 311 Live.

Planners and engineers, working together.

We're urban planners, data scientists and AI engineers. We know how city systems work and can build the technology, so we treat this as a service problem as much as an AI one.
