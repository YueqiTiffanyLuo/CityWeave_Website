Every City Problem Starts as a Story

Our Story

It Started on a Sidewalk

In the East Village, a pile of trash blocked the sidewalk. There was a school ahead, and an older neighbor in a wheelchair couldn't get past.

We opened the 311 app. The form never asked whether a wheelchair could get through. That question became 311 Live, built for the residents who are easiest to leave out.

Our Team

Planners and Engineers, Working Together

We are urban planners, data scientists and AI engineers. We understand how public systems operate, and we build the technology to improve them.

Our vision extends well beyond one product. The same engine can streamline reporting in every setting where people need to raise a problem. We want to contribute to better communities by advancing inclusion, equity and accessibility, so that every person is heard.
